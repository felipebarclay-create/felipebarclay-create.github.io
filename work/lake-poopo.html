Context

Why it exists, what it does

Problem

The filmmakers behind QOTZUÑI — an Academy Awards-qualifying documentary short — handed over years of research on the Uru communities of Bolivia as one master document: hydrology records, contamination studies, oral history, photography. No hierarchy, no entry point, no decision about what a stranger should understand first.

Solution

A reading order, not just a layout — place, then evidence, then consequence, so the argument is earned rather than announced. Data sits directly over its own photographic source, and glass panels keep the landscape visible underneath the text.
