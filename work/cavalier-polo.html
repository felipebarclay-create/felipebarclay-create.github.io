Context

A full-time role, run like a business inside a business

Role

In-house Graphic Designer, on-site, 45 hours a week — the most demanding production environment in my career so far. High client volume, tight seasonal deadlines and a catalog + collaborations to keep shipping alongside day-to-day client work.

How it actually worked

Within that structure, I ran my own client roster independently — first contact, design, revisions, delivery and payment, with no oversight from management on that process. I earned commission per client who paid, which meant every project had to actually close, not just look good.
