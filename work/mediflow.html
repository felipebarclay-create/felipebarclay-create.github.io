Context

Why it exists, what it does

Problem

Clinics run scheduling, records and day-to-day performance across disconnected tools and paper trails — nobody has a single view of how the day or the practice is actually going, and decisions get made on instinct, not data.

Solution

Six pains mapped to six modules — Today for a single daily view, Performance and Patients to bring records and indicators into one place, and an Assistant that ranks findings by priority with concrete next steps.

Research

Self-directed concept, grounded in interviews with three healthcare professionals — the director of Clínica Bessone, a dental office assistant, and a partner at Artro — to identify the actual pains a tool like this would need to solve.
