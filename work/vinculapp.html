Context

Why it exists, what it does

Problem

Digital banking assumes a confident, solo user. For older adults that breaks down — dense interfaces, small type, and a real fear of making an irreversible mistake with their own money. What they do instead is ask a relative, off the record and outside the product.

Solution

Tu Círculo — "Your Circle" — brings the help inside the app: a trusted network the user builds without ever handing over control, a persistent Help button that routes to a person, and verified transfers with a distinct, assistive view for whoever's helping.

Research & validation

University group project. We interviewed older adults and the people who help them day-to-day to map out where digital banking actually breaks down for them, which shaped the two-role structure of the app. Afterward, we tested the concept with the grandparents of our own team members — they said they'd genuinely use it if it existed.
